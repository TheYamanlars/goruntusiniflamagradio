# Crisis Cubes: Leadership Stress Test (OEB 2025 Demo Edition)

A fast, game-like artifact that blends physical play with AI-driven tension. This playbook turns the Turkish outline into a 7-minute stage-ready demo plus a 3-minute feedback sprint.

## Quick Setup (48-Hour Sprint)
- **Today (2 hours):**
  - Ask Claude: `Generate 5 micro-crisis scenarios for leadership training. Each must involve: [1] ambiguous policy, [2] conflicting stakeholder needs, [3] wellbeing vs. performance tension. Use 2025 context: AI burnout, remote-first teams, GenZ leadership transitions.`
  - Build a simple Miro board with three live metrics: **Trust**, **Transparency**, **Talent Atrophy Risk**.
  - Craft three cardboard cubes with labels.
- **Tomorrow (3 hours):**
  - Record a 1-minute 3D maze fly-through in Bezi (each turn = policy ambiguity) for use as a cutaway visual.
  - Use ElevenLabs to generate a 30-second voice clip: “Hey, that emergency demo… but tomorrow works too, right?” (anxious teammate tone).
- **Show day:**
  - Run a controlled 30-second pre-recorded gameplay clip if live volunteers are unavailable.
  - Hand each juror a paper “cube” and tally their choice via quick poll → mirror results on Miro.

## Components
- **Physical layer:** Three Decision Cubes (cardboard or 3D print)
  - **Green – Empathy:** “Private check-in; delay the project.”
  - **Blue – Speed:** “Trigger backup plan; keep shipping.”
  - **Yellow – Transparency:** “Tell the team; co-decide.”
- **Digital layer:** Miro “Team Nervous System” dashboard that updates metrics when a cube is placed.
- **AI layer:**
  - Claude: 50+ micro-crisis templates, dated December 2025, “hyper-accelerated world” tone.
  - Optional detection text: “Team picked *Speed* three times → Psychological Safety -30% → Risk: Talent Atrophy.”

## 7-Minute Live Flow (Game Show Style)
1. **Hook (min 1–2):** “Leaders keep winning, but teams burn out because tools ignore the team nervous system.”
2. **Scenario reveal:** “Teammate takes a mental health day; a customer demands a surprise demo; burnout policy is fuzzy.”
3. **Audience play (min 3–5):**
   - Invite 3 volunteers; one places a cube.
   - Update Miro metrics: Trust / Transparency / Effectiveness rise or fall.
   - Whispered AI prompt: flag repeated *Speed* picks as ethical debt.
4. **Aha (min 6–7):** “Career tools ask ‘what do you gain?’ Crisis Cubes asks ‘whom do you spend?’ Ethical debt appears as a red flag when you ‘win’ too fast.”

## 3-Minute Test & Feedback Loop
- Ask: “Which cube felt risky once you imagined the AI’s reaction?” (ethical consistency check)
- Ask: “Did the AI’s Trust metric match how you felt?” (perception vs. measurement)
- Play the 30-second ElevenLabs clip as the “silent scream” of a teammate.

## Scoring & Telemetry (lightweight)
- **Inputs:** Cube choice + scenario tag.
- **Outputs:** Metric deltas (Trust, Transparency, Talent Atrophy Risk) + AI commentary.
- **Optional rule:** Three consecutive *Speed* picks triggers a visible “Ethical Debt” warning.

## Differentiation vs. Career Dreamer (jury soundbite)
| Career Dreamer | Crisis Cubes |
|----------------|--------------|
| Individual: your career path | Collective: team nervous system |
| Outcomes: salary, title | Process: trust, psychological safety |
| Clear steps | Fuzzy policies, dilemmas |
| Rewards speed | Flags ethical inconsistency (red badge) |
